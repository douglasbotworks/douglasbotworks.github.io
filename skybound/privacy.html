Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Skybound Quest ("we", "our", or "the Application"), developed by DouglasBot Works, handles information when you use the Application on Meta Quest headsets (Meta Quest 3, Quest Pro, and Quest 2).

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Skybound Quest does not collect, sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not require you to create an account, provide your real name, email address, physical address, or phone number to play the game.

2. On-Device VR Telemetry & Flight Simulation

Skybound Quest processes 6DOF spatial head tracking and controller sensor inputs (position, orientation, analog trigger pull, and grip squeeze) exclusively in real-time on your headset to calculate flight physics and aerodynamics. None of this physical motion tracking data is transmitted off your headset or stored externally.

3. Multiplayer Gaggle Networking

When you participate in multiplayer gaggle flight sessions:

  • Cloud Room Directory: The host's chosen callsign, room name, and session connection parameters are published ephemerally to an automated room directory to allow peer discovery. Room entries are temporary and automatically erased after 45 seconds of inactivity.
  • Voice Radio (Push-To-Talk): The Application includes an optional simulated 2m VHF aviation radio. When you hold the push-to-talk button (Left Controller [Y]), voice audio is streamed in real-time to other pilots in your flight session. Audio streams are never recorded, transcribed, or stored on any server.
  • Network Protocol: Multiplayer game state (glider position, airspeed, altitude, and canopy deformation) is transmitted peer-to-peer or via Unity Relay solely to synchronize the flight simulation among active participants.

4. Third-Party Integrations

  • Meta Horizon Platform: As a Meta Quest application, platform features such as identity, entitlement checks, and system overlays are governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Spotify Web API (Optional): If you choose to connect your Spotify account for in-flight audio, authentication is performed directly via Spotify's secure OAuth 2.0 PKCE protocol. Your access token is stored strictly on your local headset in private application preferences to control music playback. We do not receive, store, or have access to your Spotify login credentials.

5. Children's Privacy

Skybound Quest complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The Application is intended for users aged 13 and older, consistent with Meta Quest hardware requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new game features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
