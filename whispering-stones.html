PROTOTYPE / PROJECT NOTE

Whispering Stones

A mystery RPG experiment combining AI-assisted NPC dialogue with an evidence collection route.

What the prototype explores

Collecting evidence, persisting checkpoints and restoring progress without replaying award events. The prototype is being reviewed for a coherent campaign and reliable gameplay.

Try a separate evidence walk

A short browser vignette uses original graphics and scripted dialogue. Visit three witnesses, gather clues and connect a shared lead. It is separate from the AI RPG and does not resolve the full mystery.

Play the free vignette ↗ · Preview instructions

Keep your case notes

Use Download checkpoint in the vignette to request a small JSON file. Check that your browser saved it before leaving; the page cannot confirm saving. If preparation fails, retry guidance appears and your investigation is preserved. Load checkpoint restores it later in the same preview, including your clues and current location. Loading replaces the investigation in the current tab. Separate load and download feedback keeps pending imports visible when you download the current case notes; downloading does not cancel a load. Cancel checkpoint load preserves the current investigation while a read is pending; a ten-second timeout offers retry guidance. Late results cannot overwrite a canceled load. No account, automatic browser storage or upload is used; reload starts fresh until you load a file.

Download the free offline preview ZIP and SHA256 checksum ↗. Serve the complete extracted folder over local HTTP using the included instructions; opening index.html through file:// is unsupported.

This release passed automated source and extracted-package tests plus separate code review. Native checkpoint file-picker/download behavior and human playtesting remain unverified. It is a free development preview, with no paid release or customer results claimed.
