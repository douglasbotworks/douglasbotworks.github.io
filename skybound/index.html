First-Principles Simulation

Built From Aerodynamic Fundamentals

Zero canned animation states or artificial rails. Everything that happens in the air emerges directly from multi-station aerodynamics and coupled pendulum dynamics.

01

Canopy-Suspended Pendulum

The aerodynamic center of lift and drag is located overhead at the canopy, while you hang 6.6m below in a speedbag pod harness, naturally swinging outward in banked turns and surging on pitch dives.

02

Convective Thermals & Ridge Lift

Hundreds of dynamic thermal columns scale with daytime solar heating, accompanied by orographic ridge lift hugging windward mountain cliffs. Lift indicators only appear when net positive climb is guaranteed.

03

4-Page Cockpit Flight Deck

A lap-mounted avionics suite featuring a radar-style minimap, digital FlyTec variometer with filtered acoustic beeper, live competition leaderboards, and wing AoA diagnostics.

04

PWCA Competition Racing

Compete in authentic Paragliding World Cup tasks with timed start cylinders, translucent waypoint pillars, and 30+ tactical NPC pilots who thermal and make cross-valley transitions.

05

Cuervo the Raven Companion

Your loyal guide bird Cuervo flies in formation with you, leads you to active thermal updrafts along the course, and glides ahead to scout optimal routes.

06

In-Game Music

Listen to the built-in soundtrack and browse available tracks from the music controls.

Intuitive Flight Controls

Natural hand controls modeled directly after authentic paraglider steering toggles and cocoon harness ergonomics.

Brakes & Steering Left / Right Trigger or Grip Pull down to deflect trailing edge brake lines. Pull both to flare and slow down; pull one side to bank and carve turns.
Weight Shift Lean Body / Tilt Head Leaning into the turn shifts weight across the carabiners, initiating smooth, energy-conserving coordinated carving.
Takeoff Launch Hold BOTH Grips for 3 Seconds A 3-second safety countdown initiates flight, automatically dismissing menu billboards for an unobstructed view.
Music Pause Menu Music Controls Browse and play the available in-game music tracks from the pause menu.
Pause & Menu Left Menu Button or Right B Brings up the large flight billboard, unfreezes simulation, and activates laser pointer rays for menu interaction.
Flight Deck Pages Point & Click Deck Tabs Switch between Nav Radar, FlyTec Vario, Competition Standings, and Wing Diagnostics on your lap instrument tray.
