How To Install on Meta Quest

Skybound Quest is distributed as a standalone APK candidate ready to install on Quest 3, Quest Pro, and Quest 2 headsets.

Method 1: SideQuest (Recommended)

  1. Download SkyboundQuest-v1.0.0.apk.
  2. Connect your Quest headset to your computer via USB (make sure Developer Mode is enabled in your Meta Horizon phone app).
  3. Open SideQuest on your PC or Mac.
  4. Click the "Install APK file from folder" button (icon with a tray and down arrow on the top toolbar).
  5. Select the downloaded APK and wait for the success notification.
  6. Put on your headset, navigate to App Library ➔ Unknown Sources, and launch Skybound Quest!

Method 2: ADB Command Line

If you have the Android Platform Tools installed, you can stream-install the APK in seconds:

adb install -r SkyboundQuest-v1.0.0.apk

To launch immediately over USB:

adb shell am start -n works.douglasbot.skybound/com.unity3d.player.UnityPlayerGameActivity

Intuitive Flight Controls

Natural hand controls modeled directly after authentic paraglider steering toggles and cocoon harness ergonomics.

Brakes & Steering Left / Right Trigger or Grip Pull down to deflect trailing edge brake lines. Pull both to flare and slow down; pull one side to bank and carve turns.
Weight Shift Lean Body / Tilt Head Leaning into the turn shifts weight across the carabiners, initiating smooth, energy-conserving coordinated carving.
Takeoff Launch Hold BOTH Grips for 3 Seconds A 3-second safety countdown initiates flight, automatically dismissing menu billboards for an unobstructed view.
In-Flight Music DJ Left Thumbstick Push Up/Down to adjust audio volume smoothly. Flick Left/Right to skip backward or forward through your playlist.
Pause & Menu Left Menu Button (≡) or Right B Brings up the large flight billboard, unfreezes simulation, and activates laser pointer rays for menu interaction.
Flight Deck Pages Point & Click Deck Tabs Switch between Nav Radar, FlyTec Vario, Competition Standings, and Wing Diagnostics on your lap instrument tray.
