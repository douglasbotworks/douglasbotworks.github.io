PROTOTYPE / PROJECT NOTE

Whispering Stones

A mystery RPG experiment combining AI-assisted NPC dialogue with an evidence collection route.

What the prototype explores

Collecting evidence, persisting checkpoints and restoring progress without replaying award events. The prototype is being reviewed for a coherent campaign and reliable gameplay.

What remains before release

Campaign completion, asset publication rights, dialogue integration and gameplay acceptance remain unresolved. There is no public playable release or purchase available here.
