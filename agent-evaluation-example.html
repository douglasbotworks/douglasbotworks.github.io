Method

Fifty synthetic gold labels were scored against stored baseline and candidate predictions using the local evaluation CLI. The saved reports were compared with an explicit maximum accuracy drop of 2 percentage points and maximum per-label recall drop of 15 percentage points.

Limits

Only these 50 fixture rows were measured. Small per-label samples produce unstable rates. Real labels, examples, and acceptance thresholds must be reviewed for a specific workflow. No latency or cost data was supplied, and this sample says nothing about live behavior.
